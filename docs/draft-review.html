At the draft

Full draft review

Your draft, checked against the interview transcript, the records, and itself. Every finding points at the sentence and the source it conflicts with. You decide what to do with it.

The review runs on a managed case. Today an invitation is a Tresa Trial Physician account; the review runs on the case you bring to it.

Request an invitation

The Skills check what is in front of you: one document, one table, one date. The review holds the whole case at once, and it is calibrated on the errors physicians actually made in signed reports.

What it checks

FamilyWhat is checkedEvidence standard
Same fact, same valueThe patient's name, date of injury, last day worked, pronouns, and causation percentages agree everywhere they appear.The draft against itself.Open
Document self-consistencyA fact left as an open question in one section is not asserted as settled in another; a denial is not affirmed elsewhere; no paragraph is repeated.The draft against itself.Managed
Draft against interviewAny fact the draft states differently from what was said in the room.The draft against the transcript, adjudicated.Managed
Empty sections and placeholdersNothing left blank or bracketed reaches a signed report.The draft.Open
Date arithmeticYears that collide, ages that do not add up, disability that starts before the last day worked, dates in the future.The draft against its own anchor dates.Open
Denials the transcript contradictsThe draft says a topic was denied when the interview addressed it.The draft against the transcript, adjudicated.Managed
Diagnosis carried throughThe diagnosis you stated, with the specifiers you gave, and not one you ruled out.The draft against what you said in the interview.Managed
Judgment stays with youP&S status, causation percentages, GAF, WPI, and statutory terms are not asserted where you did not state them.The draft against the interview.Managed
GAF near a boundaryA stated GAF close enough to a rating boundary to be worth confirming.The 2005 PDRS table.Open
Credibility left unreconciledCredibility language, exaggeration notes, and an invalid testing profile all present with no reconciliation.The draft.Managed
More than one disability periodSeveral disability periods under one diagnosis without the framing that supports it.The draft.Managed
GAF and WPI agreeThe stated GAF and WPI match the table.The 2005 PDRS table.Open
Records fidelityWhat the draft says a record says, against the record.The draft against the records.Managed
Duplicate scansThe same document scanned twice in one side's production.The records.Managed
The interview against itselfFacts stated two ways in the same interview, and whether two different people could explain it.The transcript, adjudicated and verified.Managed

Open families are the ones you can reproduce from public material; they become Skills as we go. Managed families were built and calibrated on signed reports and stay with the review.
