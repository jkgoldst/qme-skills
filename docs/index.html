Hi, I'm Josh Goldstein

Before creating MedLegal AI Hero, I spent years building and deploying AI products in highly regulated environments, including government acquisition and medical-legal work. I've worked directly with physicians, attorneys, and public-sector teams to figure out where AI is genuinely useful, and where it needs strong human oversight.

I also built Tresa, an AI platform designed around the workflow of complex medical-legal evaluations.

I'm building MedLegal AI Hero to help medical-legal experts use AI safely and effectively without giving up professional judgment.
