# Question frame

The eight groups a psychiatric QME interview must cover, in the order the memo presents them. Under each group: what the physician needs to establish, then the standard questions asked on every case. Sourced questions from the documents go above the standard ones in the memo.

The standard list below is the entire output when no documents are provided.

## 1. Mechanism and timeline of injury

Establish: what the worker says happened, when it began, whether it was one event or cumulative, when symptoms first appeared, when they were first reported, when the worker last worked.

- Describe in your own words what happened at work that you believe caused your condition. [standard question]
- When did it start? Was there a first event, or did it build up over time? [standard question]
- When did you first notice symptoms? What were they? [standard question]
- When did you first tell anyone at work? Whom, and how? [standard question]
- When did you last work? Why did you stop on that day? [standard question]
- Has anything at work changed since then (assignment, supervisor, hours, pay)? [standard question]

## 2. Work history and the Labor Code 3208.3 elements

Establish: dates of employment with this employer (the six-month element), job title and duties, hours, supervisors, personnel actions (performance reviews, discipline, investigations, terminations), and the worker's account of every workplace event they attribute their condition to, so the physician can weigh the predominant-cause and good-faith-personnel-action questions.

- When were you hired? What was your job title and what did you do day to day? [standard question]
- Did your title, duties, hours, or supervisor change while you were there? When? [standard question]
- Were you ever written up, counseled, put on a performance plan, investigated, suspended, or terminated? When, by whom, and what was the stated reason? [standard question]
- Did you file any complaint or grievance at work? What happened with it? [standard question]
- Of everything that happened at work, which events do you consider the main causes of your condition? [standard question]
- Where did you work before this employer, and why did each job end? [standard question]

## 3. Prior psychiatric history and treatment

Establish: every prior diagnosis, provider, medication, hospitalization, therapy course, and period of symptoms before the claimed injury, with dates, and what the worker was told at the time.

- Before this job, had you ever seen a psychiatrist, psychologist, therapist, or counselor? When, for what, and for how long? [standard question]
- Had you ever been prescribed medication for mood, anxiety, sleep, or attention? Which, by whom, and for how long? [standard question]
- Were you taking any such medication in the year before your symptoms at work began? [standard question]
- Have you ever been hospitalized for a psychiatric reason or gone to an emergency room for one? [standard question]
- Have you ever had thoughts of harming yourself or attempted to? When? [standard question]
- How were you doing emotionally in the year before the work problems began? [standard question]
- Do any blood relatives have a psychiatric diagnosis, substance problem, or suicide? [standard question]
- How much alcohol do you drink, and did that change? Any drug use, prescribed or not? [standard question]

## 4. Non-industrial stressors

Establish: what else was happening in the worker's life before, during, and since the claimed injury period: relationships, family, children, finances, housing, legal matters, immigration, health of self and family, deaths, caregiving.

- During the period your symptoms developed, what else was going on in your life outside work? [standard question]
- Any separation, divorce, or relationship conflict in the last several years? When? [standard question]
- Any serious illness, injury, or death in your family or close friends? When? [standard question]
- Any financial difficulty, debt, bankruptcy, eviction, or foreclosure? [standard question]
- Any legal matters (criminal, civil, custody, immigration) other than this claim? [standard question]
- Are you caring for a child, parent, or other relative? [standard question]
- Any physical health problems of your own, and how do they affect you day to day? [standard question]

## 5. Activities of daily living, before and after

Establish: a concrete picture of function before the injury and now, in the worker's own words, across sleep, self-care, household, driving, shopping, finances, social life, recreation, sexual function, and concentration.

- Walk me through a typical day now, from waking to sleep. [standard question]
- Walk me through a typical day before the work problems began. [standard question]
- How is your sleep: time to bed, time to sleep, awakenings, time up, naps? [standard question]
- Who does the cooking, cleaning, laundry, shopping, and bills in your home? Did that change? [standard question]
- Do you drive? Any change in how far or how often? [standard question]
- Whom do you see socially, and how often? How does that compare to before? [standard question]
- What did you do for enjoyment before, and what do you do now? [standard question]
- Any change in appetite or weight, sexual interest or function, or ability to concentrate on reading, television, or conversation? [standard question]

## 6. Current treatment and medications

Establish: every current provider, the frequency of visits, every current medication with dose, who prescribes it, adherence, side effects, and what the worker believes has helped.

- Who are you seeing now for your emotional condition, how often, and since when? [standard question]
- What medications are you taking now, at what dose, and who prescribes each? [standard question]
- Do you take them every day as prescribed? Any side effects? [standard question]
- What has helped most? What has not helped? [standard question]
- Have any providers told you a diagnosis? What did they say? [standard question]
- Who is your primary care physician? [standard question]

## 7. Prior claims and litigation

Establish: every prior workers' compensation claim, personal injury claim, disability claim, or lawsuit, with dates, body parts or conditions, outcome, and any prior medical-legal evaluation.

- Have you ever filed a workers' compensation claim before this one? When, for what, and how was it resolved? [standard question]
- Have you ever filed a personal injury, disability, or unemployment claim, or been a party to a lawsuit? [standard question]
- Have you ever been evaluated by a QME, AME, or other physician for a claim or lawsuit before? [standard question]
- Are you receiving any benefits now (temporary disability, state disability, unemployment, Social Security)? [standard question]

## 8. What the records say that the worker should confirm or dispute

This group has no standard questions. It exists only when documents were provided, and holds the questions that put a record statement to the worker: a statement attributed to the worker, a prior evaluator's finding, or a conflict between two documents (a date the referral asserts that the records place elsewhere, two providers recording different histories). A record fact that opens a topic (a medication, a divorce, a prior claim) belongs in that topic's group instead.

Each question quotes or points to the record statement and asks the worker to confirm, correct, date, or explain it. It states nothing about which side is right.

Pattern: `Dr. X's note of DATE records "[verbatim, at most 25 words]" [Doc, p. N]. Is that accurate? What do you recall?`
